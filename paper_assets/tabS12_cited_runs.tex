Each run header gives the run number and the data of the run; a row that spans the columns states a result in words. Acc./false: accepted / false improvements, with the false-improvement share in parentheses (\%). Bound: KL-ratio bound ($b=1$), \%. LOCO: leave-one-dataset-out bound, \%. Yield: accepted updates per $100$ decisions. Superscripts. Power check LOCO$_0$, the LOCO bound recomputed with zero false improvements: a, $0.81\%$; b, $18.22\%$; c, $5.54\%$. With $50$ trials per dataset the bound exceeds $10\%$ even when no decision is a false improvement, and $100$ or more trials bring it below $10\%$. d: $95\%$ upper bound of the false-improvement rate, from a bootstrap over the $11$ datasets with 10,000 resamples, \%. e: estimate minus reference truth for the deployed configuration, against the margin $0.005$. f: LOCO by source. g: Proposition~\ref{prop:bound} bound on $\mathcal{Q}$. h: three Monte Carlo checks, namely the standard deviation of the 5\% bootstrap quantile over 20 bootstrap seeds per trial, the share of re-runs whose decision one alternative seed changes, and the share of trials in which at least one of the 20 seeds changes the decision. i: range of the per-dataset median bootstrap standard error of the reference gain, separately for references of 43,634 to 100,000 rows ($\ge$ 40,000) and 468 to 1,143 rows ($<$ 40,000). MINI-12 and MINI-14 tune HGB with Optuna (tree-structured Parzen estimator, 25 search iterations) on the 630-row SEARCH split; MINI-14 repeats this at 630, 2,000 and 5,000 tuning rows. MINI-33b: share of tasks on which the regularized XGBoost has a lower base weighted-$F_1$ than HGB on the locked reference.
